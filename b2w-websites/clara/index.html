CLARA / COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE · PRODUCT VISION

Start with what you see on site. End with a clearer plan everyone can work from. One continuous renovation workflow.

Describe a property repair in a voice note. Turn it into a preliminary estimate, verify it with others, develop the scope, explore design directions, and assemble the material for a decision.

Explore the workflow ↗Discuss Clara

From observation to decision

Less re-entering the same information. More time making the right decision.

Designed for property owners, investors, and the people bringing commercial spaces back to life. Clara is a product concept: the walkthrough below is illustrative, not a live estimating engine.

01 / CAPTURE

Talk through the site.

Record what needs repairing and the condition of the property while you walk it.

02 / VERIFY

Get a grounded estimate.

Organize work items and assumptions, then invite contractors and collaborators to verify real costs.

03 / SCOPE

Define what gets done.

Turn observations and verified decisions into an editable project scope.

04 / ENVISION

See possibilities.

Use AI-assisted concept conversations to explore how a renovation could look.

05 / ORGANIZE

Keep one project record.

Store scopes, visual concepts, estimates, and review decisions together.

06 / PRESENT

Bring everyone along.

Package the project into presentation materials for investors, teams, and builders.

Built for shared decisions

A project is more than its estimate.

Owners need to know what work is needed. Investors need to understand cost and intended outcomes. Builders need a scope they can review and execute. Clara's planned collaboration experience is designed to keep those views grounded in the same evolving project record.

ONE SOURCE OF PROJECT CONTEXT

Keep the estimate, scope, concepts, and feedback together.

Every document should communicate its status, provenance, and revision. A preliminary concept should never be mistaken for a contractor-verified estimate or an approved construction scope.
