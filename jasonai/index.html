JASONAI / FOR GENERAL CONTRACTOR OWNERS

You run the business. Jason keeps track of everything happening around it. Your executive secretary, wherever you already message.

Share authorized WhatsApp conversations and text updates. Jason organizes project information into memory, identifies what deserves attention, and brings it into a dashboard you can work from.

Try the operations demo ↗Ask about a pilot

From messages to decisions

No new habit for your crew. More clarity for the owner.

Jason is designed to support the person responsible for the business. It isn't a replacement for project managers or field teams.
